What is EraX (ERAX)?

EraX (ERAX) is the native ecosystem token of EraX Digital. It is designed to support ecosystem participation, staking incentives, community growth, governance, and platform-related economic activities.

The token is developed using Solidity smart contracts and the Hardhat development framework.

What is the total supply of ERAX?

The proposed maximum total supply is:

1,000,000,000 ERAX (1 billion tokens).

The supply is designed to be fixed, with no additional minting after the initial token issuance.

What is the vesting schedule for ERAX?

The proposed vesting schedule is designed to encourage long-term participation and responsible token distribution.

  • Community & Ecosystem: 10% at TGE, remaining 90% over 36 months.
  • Staking & Rewards: Distributed over 8 years according to the reward emission schedule.
  • Foundation & Treasury: 10% at TGE, remaining 90% over 48 months.
  • Team & Contributors: 12-month cliff, followed by 36-month linear vesting.
  • Private Investors: 10% at TGE, 6-month cliff, followed by 36-month linear vesting.
  • Partnerships & Marketing: 10% at TGE, remaining 90% over 36 months.
