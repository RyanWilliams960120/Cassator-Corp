01 — Strategic Foundation

Completed. Established the company's product vision, independent positioning, technical direction, and initial ecosystem strategy, including company positioning, product requirements, the initial SocialFi architecture, an Ethereum Mainnet and Base network strategy, and security, scalability, and infrastructure principles.

02 — Core Infrastructure

Completed. Established the foundational technical architecture required to support the EraX Digital ecosystem, including backend architecture, an authentication and user identity framework, account and profile foundations, wallet connectivity architecture, database and API infrastructure, and foundational security and access-control design.

03 — SocialFi Platform

Upcoming. Develop the core social experience connecting users, communities, and digital ownership. Planned capabilities include user registration and customizable profiles, social feeds and content publishing, likes, comments, and social interactions, community creation, private messaging, and wallet-connected user experiences.

04 — Creator Economy

Planned. Introduce tools that help creators build communities and develop monetization opportunities. Planned capabilities include creator profiles and content management, paid content and subscriptions, creator tipping and digital payments, smart-contract integration on Base, on-chain payment infrastructure, and creator and community engagement tools.

05 — Security, Testing & Beta Launch

Planned. Prepare the platform for controlled testing and a potential beta release. Planned activities include smart-contract testing and validation, security assessments, backend performance and reliability testing, monitoring and operational readiness, closed beta preparation, and product refinement.

06 — Ecosystem Growth & Expansion

Future Development. Explore additional capabilities to expand the EraX Digital ecosystem over time. Potential areas include advanced creator and community tools, enhanced wallet functionality, potential rewards and staking mechanisms, cross-chain interoperability, digital asset and tokenization capabilities, and infrastructure scalability.
