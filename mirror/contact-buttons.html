Work began on new SL8 apps and web version. Due to Russia's full-scale aggression against Ukraine, costs were significantly cut, the team was reduced, and part of it was relocated to the EU.

Wefunder, Stellar nodes, Gold Eagle, trademarks, token holders from Japan, revenue growth, team expansion

SEC filings and launch of a test campaign on the Wefunder crowdfunding platform to attract investment. Our nodecloud became the largest on the Stellar distributed ledger. Global Influencers, Pivot to RWA Platform, EU Subsidiary, Content Monetization, Revenue Growth

Signing letters of intent with global mega-influencers. SL8 pivots toward the RWA platform and adds corresponding functionality. An EU subsidiary with a VASP license is registered. SL8 evolves to include additional content monetization features. Revenue doubles again compared to the previous year.

Brand Ambassadors, Launch of First RWA Token, RWA Platform Expansion and Scaling, First Major Media Mentions, Preparing to Raise Investment

Signing the first contracts with influencer brand ambassadors. The first version of the token launch agreement has been developed. We are mentioned for the first time in publications at the level of Forbes and USA Today. Cassator Corp is preparing to raise investment.
